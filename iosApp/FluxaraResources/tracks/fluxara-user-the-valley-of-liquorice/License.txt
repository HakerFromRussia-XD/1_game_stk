The Valley of Liquorice is a modification of "The Valley of Ice" by Frode 魔大农 Lindeijer / Modanung. 

Original license for "The Valley of Ice": CC-BY-SA

------------------------------------------
Changes done by RX1 (rx1@posteo.de):
- textures were modified
- replaced music
- new objects with new textures and gloss maps
	- track borders
	- speed boost zipper arrows
	- direction pointers
	- new ice surface, with new cracks and water effects
- scripting functions and triggers were implemented / added 
	- to increase or randomize the racing speed each lap / phase
	- to play sounds and write messages
	- for direction and phase-specific track alterations
- new lights
- modifications of existing lights
- minor landscape modifications
- scene-XML file modification: custom ("snow") particles
- reverse ramp
- new drivelines
- corrections of existing drivelines
- transparent crystals in different colors
